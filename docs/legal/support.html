2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I join my academic section?
Your class representative will provide a 6-character Section Join Code (e.g., BSCS26) or a direct QR code. Tap "Join Section" on the Agenda tab and enter the code.

Does ClassSync work when I am offline?
Yes. ClassSync uses a native dual-tier database architecture (SQLite on-device + Supabase cloud). Your entire schedule is cached locally and is 100% accessible even in lecture basements without cellular reception.

How do notifications work?
Instant push notifications are delivered for room changes, cancellations, and makeup classes. You can configure "Smart Quiet Hours" in the Settings tab to suppress non-urgent alerts during sleep hours.

3. Requesting Account Deletion (Without Device Access)

Direct Account Deletion URL & Instructions:

If you have the ClassSync app installed, you can delete your account instantly by navigating to: Settings → Destructive Actions → Delete Account & Data.

If you no longer have access to your mobile device or have uninstalled the application, you may request permanent erasure by sending an email from your registered account email to support@classsync.app with the subject line "ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST". Our team will verify ownership and permanently erase your account and associated attendance data within 48 hours.
