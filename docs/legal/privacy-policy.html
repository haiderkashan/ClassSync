Privacy Notice

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 | Application: ClassSync (iOS & Android)

ClassSync ("we", "our", or "us") provides a real-time academic timetable and cohort schedule coordination platform. We are committed to transparency regarding how student and class representative information is collected, stored, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable schedule synchronization and attendance analytics, we collect only the minimum necessary data:

  • Account & Identity Data: When signing in with Google or Apple, we receive your verified user ID, email address, and display name managed via Clerk Auth.
  • Academic & Cohort Information: Section memberships, course enrollments, personalized timetable preferences, and attendance history logs.
  • Push Notification Device Tokens: APNs (Apple) or FCM (Android) push tokens strictly utilized to transmit time-critical academic schedule alerts, room changes, and deadline reminders.
  • Local Cache & Diagnostic Data: Offline SQLite local replicas of your schedule and error logs necessary for resilient synchronization.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect (Zero Cross-App Tracking)

Zero Tracking Guarantee: As declared in our Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), ClassSync does NOT participate in cross-app tracking, does not sell user data to data brokers, and does not serve targeted third-party advertising.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Data Processing

ClassSync uses industry-standard, SOC-2 compliant service providers to run our infrastructure:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL & Storage): Encrypted database host located in secure AWS data centers with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement.
  • Clerk Auth: Cryptographically secure identity management and OAuth token issuance.
  • Expo Application Services (EAS) & Apple APNs / Google FCM: Encrypted push notification delivery pipelines.

4. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure (Apple Guideline 5.1.1v)

Permanent Data Deletion: You have the absolute right to delete your ClassSync account and erase all associated data at any time directly within the application:
  1. Navigate to the Settings tab in the ClassSync app.
  2. Scroll down to the Destructive Actions section.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Data and type "DELETE" to confirm.

This immediately and irreversibly cascades deletion across your database profile, attendance logs, personal tasks, push tokens, and Clerk authentication credentials.

Note for Genesis Class Representatives: To prevent catastrophic disruption to your cohort, Genesis CRs must transfer cohort administrative ownership to a Co-Admin before initiating account deletion.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request manual account deletion, please reach out to our privacy engineering team:
